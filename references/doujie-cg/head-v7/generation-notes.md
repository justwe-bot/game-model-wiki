# Doujie CG Head V7

V7 is a source-level correction after V6 was rejected in Hunyuan Web V3.1.
The mouth is intentionally flatter than the final desired appearance because
the generator inflated V6 lips. Hair uses broad smooth masses with restrained
strand variation to avoid sculpted grooves and oily highlight bands.

Material acceptance targets after download:

- skin roughness: 0.72-0.82
- lip roughness: 0.60-0.72
- hair roughness: 0.62-0.75
- specular IOR level: approximately 0.10-0.18
- normal strength: no more than 0.10 on the face and hair unless visually proven

Reject thick lips, a deep mouth slit, gray or blown-out eyes, long neck,
shoulders, carved hair grooves or clear-coat/oily highlights.
